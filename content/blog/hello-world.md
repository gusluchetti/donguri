Welcome to the donguri blog. This is where we'll share updates about the project — new features, design decisions, and the occasional deep-dive into our stack.

## What is donguri?

donguri is a personal catalog for movies, TV shows, anime, and games. It's built on open data (Wikidata, TMDB, TVMaze) and supports [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) for portable, user-owned data.

## What's next

We have a lot planned — episode tracking, genre filtering, data import/export, and more. Check out the [roadmap](/roadmap) to see what's coming.

Stay tuned.
