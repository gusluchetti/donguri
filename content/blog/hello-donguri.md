Welcome to the donguri blog. This is where we'll share updates, dev notes, and the occasional ramble about what we're building and why.

## What is donguri?

A personal media catalog. Movies, TV, anime, games — tracked your way, stored on your terms. Built on open protocols (AT Protocol for Bluesky users), no algorithmic feeds, no engagement farming.

## Why a blog?

We wanted a low-friction way to share what's shipping. No CMS, no database — just markdown files that get bundled at build time. Each deploy can have a post. Or not. No pressure.

## What's next

Check the [roadmap](/roadmap) for the full picture. The short version: better episode tracking, genre filtering, MAL import, and more.

Thanks for being here.
